# ONE group for every crawler, on purpose. A crawler obeys only the most
# specific group naming it, so the old per-bot "User-agent: Googlebot /
# Allow: /" groups made Google and every other named bot skip the Disallow
# lines under "*" entirely (/admin was being served to Googlebot). Naming the
# bots inside the shared group keeps them explicitly welcome for AI and search
# visibility without that side effect. Keep the User-agent lines contiguous:
# some parsers end a group at a blank line.
#
# Google (search, Gemini) · OpenAI · Anthropic · Perplexity · Apple ·
# Amazon · Meta · Microsoft · You.com · Cohere · ByteDance
